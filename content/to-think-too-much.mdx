I think there’s something deeply wrong with people who think too much.

And I don’t mean intelligent people. I mean people who can’t seem to leave themselves alone.

People who can turn the smallest thing into something that keeps them awake at night.

A conversation from three years ago. A decision they made yesterday. Something someone said, and all the things they *might* have meant by it.

And the worst part is, you can’t even explain why you’re doing it.

You tell yourself you’re trying to understand things. Understand people. Understand yourself.

But at what point does trying to understand your life become a way of avoiding actually living it?

I think about Dostoevsky sometimes. He wrote about people who were so painfully aware of themselves that they almost became prisoners of their own minds.

And I wonder if that’s what happens when you spend too much time looking inward.

You start questioning everything.

Your intentions. Your feelings. Whether you really love someone, or whether you just love the feeling of being loved.

Whether you’re a good person, or whether you’ve simply never been put in a position that reveals otherwise.

And even when you’re happy, there’s this little voice asking you why.

*Why are you happy? What does this mean? Will it last?*

It’s almost as if you can’t experience something without immediately trying to understand it.

You can’t just be sad. You have to understand the sadness.

You can’t just love someone. You have to understand the love.

You can’t just exist.

You have to make sense of your existence.

And somewhere along the way, I think you start losing the ability to experience things as they are.

Everything becomes something to analyse. Something to dissect. Something to find meaning in.

And maybe that’s the cruelest part of being so self-aware.

You can understand exactly why you’re unhappy and still have no idea how to be happy.

You can recognise every mistake you’re making and continue making it.

You can spend hours thinking about the person you want to become, only to realise you’ve spent another day doing nothing to become that person.

And the strange thing is, you almost feel productive while doing it.

Because thinking feels like progress.

Understanding feels like progress.

But sometimes, you’re just standing in the same place, looking at it from a hundred different angles.

I wonder how much of my life I’ve spent doing that.

How many moments I’ve ruined by thinking about them while they were happening.

How many things I’ve never done because I was too busy imagining how they might go wrong.

How many versions of myself I’ve created in my head, without ever allowing any of them to exist in the real world.

And I don’t know.

Maybe some people are just like this.

Maybe we were never meant to understand everything about ourselves.

Maybe some things are supposed to happen without us knowing exactly why.

Maybe you can love someone without understanding what it says about you.

Maybe you can be happy without wondering whether you deserve it.

Maybe you can make a terrible decision and just let it be a terrible decision, without turning it into a statement about who you are as a person.

I don’t know.

I just think it’s strange that we spend so much time trying to understand what it means to be alive…

that sometimes, we forget to be alive.

And I wonder if I’ll ever learn how to stop thinking about my life long enough to actually live it.
